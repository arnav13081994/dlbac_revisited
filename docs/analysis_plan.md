# Analysis plan (pre-specified before the runs)

This is the plan text as fixed on 20 Oct 2026, before any of the new experiments was run, with the amendments that followed. E1, E3 and E4 were planned before the runs. E5, E5c and E6 were added afterwards in response to the first results (Amendments 7 and 8) and are marked as such; they were written down before their own jobs started. The plan is kept as written; do not edit it. Results are in `results/REPORT_e1.txt` ... `REPORT_e6.txt`.

The paper reports the same runs with the DLBAC paper's own practice (F1 at threshold 0.5, paired t-test over the eight datasets, p < 0.05) and adds the Holm-adjusted p-value for the family of comparisons; the bootstrap intervals and equivalence tests listed below are in the reports in `results/` but not in the paper. E4 (column-order ablation) is in the repository but not discussed in the paper. The descriptive analyses listed as "tier C" were not carried out. Amendments 5 and 6 (about earlier exploratory runs and about tooling) are omitted here; the remaining numbers are kept.

**Hypothesis to be tested.** On the authors' own datasets, protocol and metrics, standard classical models given the user=resource equality structure that the paper's own Section 4.1 says generates the labels (rule constraints such as department=department) are not worse than the authors' ResNet by more than 0.01 F1, and the ResNet's advantage over the paper's baselines does not survive standard baselines. We commit to reporting the result whichever way it falls, including cases where the ResNet wins.

**Fixed for every new experiment**

- Data: the authors' eight synthetic datasets (u4k-r4k-auth11k to u6k-r6k-auth32k); Amazon is not used. Paper protocol: the columns the released code exposes (user fields 0-7, last 8 resource fields). Metrics as in the released code: F1, FPR, TPR, precision pooled over the four operations at threshold 0.5.
- "Their model": the authors' ResNet as released: depth 8 for u4k-r4k-auth11k and u5k-r5k-auth12k, depth 56 for the others; 60 / 30 epochs; batch 16; Adam; the released step learning-rate schedule; L2 1e-4 on convolution kernels; sigmoid outputs, binary cross-entropy; final-epoch weights; no tuning by us. Implemented in PyTorch with TensorFlow-style asymmetric `same` padding and used only after E2 validates it. The authors' script evaluates on the test set every epoch but never selects on it, so final-epoch weights are the faithful choice.
- "Updated" models: random forest, gradient boosting (scikit-learn HistGradientBoosting) and a one-hot MLP, each without and with the 64 equality indicators 1\[user field i == resource field j\]. Equal tuning budget: 12 configurations each, chosen by pooled F1 on a 20% validation split of the training set, then refit on all of it. RF: n\_estimators {200, 500} x max\_features {sqrt, 0.5, 1.0} x min\_samples\_leaf {1, 2}. GB: learning rate {0.05, 0.1, 0.2} x max\_leaf\_nodes {31, 63} x l2 {0, 1}, 500 iterations with early stopping. MLP: hidden {4x128, 256-128, 64-64} x activation {relu, tanh} x alpha {1e-4, 1e-2}. The paper's own baselines are also run in their configuration (RF defaults, SVM on standardised codes, MLP 4x128 on raw codes).
- ResNet+eq: the authors' network with the 64 indicators concatenated to its pooled 64-dimensional feature vector before the output layer; nothing else changed.
- Statistics: unit = dataset (n = 8), mean over seeds; paired differences; 95% bootstrap CI (10,000 resamples over datasets); equivalence by two one-sided tests; Holm correction over each pre-listed family. Every run saves per-decision predictions.

**E1 (tier A): the comparison on the authors' own split.** 8 datasets, seeds 0-4 for every model. Family of 10 comparisons: ResNet minus each of RF, SVM, MLP raw (all in the paper's configuration), tuned RF, tuned GB, tuned MLP, RF+eq, GB+eq, MLP+eq; and ResNet+eq minus ResNet. Equivalence margin 0.01 F1 (sensitivity 0.005 and 0.02); the margin is a judgement, about 7x the ResNet's seed SD (0.0014) and a quarter of the spread across datasets (0.04). Predictions: ResNet better than RF, SVM and MLP raw (their claims); equivalent to tuned GB and tuned MLP; each equality-augmented classical model equivalent or better. A model's prediction is falsified if the 95% CI of (ResNet minus model) lies above 0.01 after Holm. No prediction for ResNet+eq: if it improves, the ResNet had not extracted the relation; if not, it already encodes it.

**E2 (tier A): port validation.** Load the authors' shipped weights for u4k-r4k-auth11k (the file whose log is `neural_network_training/results/result.txt`: TN 4746, FP 87, FN 50, TP 3885) into our PyTorch network and evaluate their test file. Pass if per-decision agreement with the authors' evaluation is at least 99.9% and the confusion counts differ by at most 9 decisions of 8,768. If it fails, every ResNet result is labelled unvalidated. We also report the second shipped network (decision engine) and our retrained networks.

**E3 (tier B): generalisation to unseen entities.** For each dataset and split seed 0, 1, 2: pool the authors' train and test files; hold out a random 20% of user ids and a random 20% of resource ids; train on tuples whose user and resource are both not held out, keeping a random 15% of them as a warm test set; test sets: warm, user-cold (user held out, resource seen), resource-cold, both-cold. Models: ResNet, ResNet+eq, and tuned RF, GB, MLP each without and with eq; model seed = split seed. Sets with fewer than 50 tuples or one class are excluded and listed. Family of 12: ResNet minus each equality-augmented classical model in each cold condition (9), and ResNet+eq minus ResNet in each (3); margin 0.02 (sensitivity 0.01 and 0.03). Prediction: all models do worse cold than warm; none on ordering. If the ResNet beats the augmented models cold, we say so and restrict the thesis to the authors' random split.

**E4 (tier B): layout ablation in the authors' protocol.** u4k-r4k-auth11k and u5k-r5k-auth12k (the paper's depth-8 datasets), seeds 0-4; the authors' ResNet-8 and the paper's MLP configuration on one-hot input. Conditions on the one-hot grid: none; same (one random permutation of value columns applied identically to every field row, so equal values stay aligned); user (independent permutations for user rows and resource rows, so equal values no longer share a column); fields (one random permutation of the 16 field rows). Predictions: ResNet F1 under user is below none by more than 0.02; under same within 0.01 of none; the MLP changes by less than 0.01 in every condition (it is invariant to fixed feature permutations by construction); no prediction for fields. If the ResNet drop under user is below 0.02, the representation argument is dropped for the paper's protocol.

**After the first draft (tier C, descriptive only):** error overlap between models and errors by number of equal pairs, from the saved predictions.

**Disclosed departures from the authors' setup:** PyTorch instead of Keras; the one-hot width is the maximum over train and test together (the authors encode each file with its own maximum; we check that the widths agree); E3 pools their train and test files; the tuned baselines get a 12-configuration budget that the ResNet does not (it uses the authors' own recipe).

**Deviations log:** no change to the models, grids, seeds, margins or families after the first run of the new experiments. Jobs that were interrupted were re-run from scratch with the same seeds.

**Amendments and clarifications (20 Oct 2026, after the plan was fixed and before any E1, E3 or E4 run)**

1. E2's per-decision agreement clause cannot be measured because the authors ship no per-decision predictions; the pass criterion is the confusion counts.
2. RF and GB use the raw integer codes (plus the 64 indicators); the one-hot MLP uses one-hot codes (plus the indicators).
3. The authors' per-file one-hot widths equal ours on all eight datasets (checked), so that disclosed departure is void.
4. The depth rule is by dataset (depth 8 for the two datasets with 8+8 metadata, else 56), which equals the authors' rule (depth 56 whenever metadata are hidden).

**Amendment 7 (21 Oct 2026, written after the E1, E3 and E4 results were read and before any E5 or E6 job was run): follow-up experiments E5 and E6.** Both are post hoc in motivation, because they respond to results. They are reported as follow-ups, separately from the pre-specified E1, E3 and E4 families, which are not changed. Hypotheses, families, margins and decision rules are fixed here. "Exceeds" below means the mean difference is above the stated margin and its Holm-adjusted difference test is significant at 0.05.

- **E5: can the ResNet use the equality indicators if its output layer can combine them non-linearly?** Variants: ResNet+eqH = the authors' network whose 64 pooled features and 64 indicators go through one hidden layer of 128 ReLU units before the output layer; ResNetH = the same head without the indicators (capacity control). Everything else is the authors' recipe (depth rule, epochs, schedule, L2 on convolution kernels only, final-epoch weights). This is a non-linear late fusion, not fusion inside the grid, because pair-specific indicators have no natural position in the one-hot grid; an in-grid design was not tried. Authors' split: 8 datasets, seeds 0-4, both variants. Cold splits: ResNet+eqH only, split seeds 0-2. Why: the ResNet+eq null in E1 and E3 was read in the pre-specification as "it already encodes the relation", yet the ResNet scores like the relation-blind models; the hypothesis is that its linear output layer could not use the indicators. Authors'-split family (Holm, margin 0.01, 6 comparisons): ResNet+eqH minus ResNet, ResNet+eqH minus ResNetH, ResNetH minus ResNet, and RF+eq, GB+eq and MLP+eq each minus ResNet+eqH. Cold family (Holm, margin 0.02, 12 comparisons): ResNet+eqH minus each of RF+eq, GB+eq, MLP+eq and ResNet in each of the three cold conditions.
- **E5 decision rules.** (a) If the linear head was the limit: ResNet+eqH minus ResNet exceeds 0.01, ResNet+eqH minus ResNetH exceeds 0.01, and GB+eq minus ResNet+eqH does not exceed 0.01. Then the paper replaces its ResNet+eq entry by ResNet+eqH and withdraws "adding the indicators does not help the ResNet". (b) If ResNet+eqH minus ResNet does not exceed 0.01: the limit is the convolutional trunk or the training recipe, not the linear head, and the paper says the ResNet could not use the relation under the authors' recipe with either head (one fusion design only). (c) If ResNetH minus ResNet exceeds 0.01: head capacity alone helps, and (a) cannot be read as an equality effect. (d) Cold splits: no prediction on the ordering; we report it whichever way it falls.
- **E6: is the gap to the paper's SVM an encoding effect?** Tuned RBF SVM on sparse one-hot input, without (svm\_tuned) and with (svm\_tuned\_eq) the 64 indicators. 12 configurations: C in {1, 10, 100} and gamma = g divided by the mean number of non-zeros per row, g in {0.5, 1, 2, 4}; chosen on the same 20% validation split of the training set, then refit; hard 0/1 decisions as for the paper's SVM. Authors' split seeds 0-4, cold split seeds 0-2. Authors'-split family (Holm, margin 0.01, 2): ResNet minus svm\_tuned and ResNet minus svm\_tuned\_eq. Cold family (Holm, margin 0.02, 6): the same two in each cold condition. Prediction: svm\_tuned is within 0.01 of the tuned one-hot MLP (0.938) and svm\_tuned\_eq is close to MLP+eq (0.948), so ResNet minus svm\_tuned lies between -0.01 and +0.01 and ResNet minus svm\_tuned\_eq is negative. Decision rule: if the lower CI bound of ResNet minus svm\_tuned is above 0.01 after Holm, the paper's SVM gap partly survives tuning and the paper says so.
- Models, code path and seeds for every other group are as before; interrupted jobs are re-run from scratch as before. Results go to folders `f1` and `f3` under `results/`, reports to `REPORT_e5.txt` and `REPORT_e6.txt` (script `src/dlbac_revisited/analyze.py`, self-check `tests/test_analyze.py`).

**Amendment 8 (22 Oct 2026, written after the E5 authors'-split results were read and before any E5c job was run): E5c, the head-only control on the cold splits.** On the authors' split, E5 found that ResNetH (hidden layer, no indicators) improves on the ResNet by 0.0101, exactly as much as ResNet+eqH does (+0.0101; ResNet+eqH minus ResNetH is -0.0000 and equivalent). The cold-split part of E5 ran only ResNet+eqH, so its cold gain over the ResNet cannot yet be attributed to the head or to the indicators. E5c runs ResNetH on the cold splits: 8 datasets, split seeds 0-2, 24 jobs, the same cells and seeds as the ResNet+eqH cold runs. Family (Holm, margin 0.02, 6 comparisons): ResNet+eqH minus ResNetH and ResNetH minus ResNet in each of the three cold conditions. Prediction, from the authors'-split result: ResNet+eqH minus ResNetH is equivalent to zero at margin 0.02 in all three conditions, and ResNetH minus ResNet is positive. Decision rule: if ResNet+eqH minus ResNetH is equivalent at 0.02 in all three conditions, the cold gain is attributed to head capacity, not to the indicators; if it exceeds 0.02 in any condition, the indicators help on unseen entities and the paper says so. Results go to `results/f3` (group `resnet_head`), and the report is the E5c family inside `REPORT_e5.txt`.
